# 30-second product moment
1. Open the black or white BridgeVoice chat. Keep the sidebar collapsed for presentation.
2. Click Try a 30-second Meaning Check. Say: “A translation can sound fluent and still lose an important condition.”
3. Choose Try a deliberate omission, then Check important details. Point out missing negation and the condition.
4. Correct the wording; check again. Explain that no detected difference is not a guarantee.
5. With a tested Meta connection and consent, use Ask Meta to review meaning for semantic assistance. Otherwise clearly show the local demo only.
6. Close the example and use the waveform button for Voice conversation. Show a real transcript, correction, sending and an installed supported voice reading a reply. Rehearse on the actual presentation device.

Pitch: “BridgeVoice helps people explain important information back in their own words, so a human can catch what changed before relying on it.”
